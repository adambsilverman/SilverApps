Chord Progression Lab

Build a progression from Roman numerals, then hear it in any key with different voicings, accompaniment patterns, a bass line and a drum beat. Because the numerals describe chords by scale degree, changing the key moves every chord at once. Learn the labels in Introduction to Roman Numeral Analysis.

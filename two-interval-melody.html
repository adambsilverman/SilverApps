Two-Interval Melody

Compose a four-bar melody with no key signature, in 4/4 or 12/8, using only two different-sized intervals (for example a minor 2nd and a major 3rd) from each note to the next. Change direction often and be creative with rhythm. Your melody will probably be highly chromatic! Each interval is labeled above the staff, between its two notes, and the tool writes the clef, time signature and stems for you.

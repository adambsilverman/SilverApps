Key ideas
  1. Pedals: D C B (left foot) | E F G A (right foot). Top notch = flat, middle = natural, bottom = sharp, in every octave.
  2. Spell for the pedals: A♯ and B♭ are different strings; never write double sharps or flats. Two pedal changes at once only with opposite feet.
  3. Glissandos: any seven-note scale, or 7th chords using enharmonic doublings; triads can't be glissed.
  4. Harmonics: a circle over the written note; they sound an octave higher, best at mf or softer.

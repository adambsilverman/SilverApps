Multitrack Studio

Build a song in layers. Start with a backing track (send one from the Chord Progression Lab or open an audio file), then add tracks: play a melody on a synth with the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard, or record yourself through the microphone. Mix the tracks and export the result. Everything stays on your device; export your song to keep it.

Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; play C up to E (white keys on the middle row, black keys above). Z and X change the octave. Notes play on the selected synth track (or a piano if none is selected).

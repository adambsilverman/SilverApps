Rhythm Performance

Read the rhythm, listen to the count-in, then perform it. Practice generated rhythms, rhythms from around the world, classical dance rhythms and drum rudiments, clap on cue while counting rests, or perform a phasing piece in the manner of Steve Reich's Clapping Music. Perform by tapping the pad or the space bar, or by clapping into the microphone. The app shows which notes were on time, early, late, missed or extra.

Key ideas
  1. Phrase: a complete musical thought ending with a cadence; label a, a′, b.
  2. Period: antecedent (open or weak) + consequent (closed); parallel or contrasting, balanced or asymmetrical, possibly progressive.
  3. Phrase group: phrases that belong together without antecedent/consequent (for example, both closed).
  4. Sentence: presentation (idea + repetition) and continuation to a cadence.

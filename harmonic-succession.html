Key ideas
  1. Functions: tonic (I), pre-dominant (IV, ii), dominant (V, vii°). T → PD → D → T.
  2. Chart: iii → vi → IV/ii → V/vii° → I; I goes anywhere. Exceptions: IV–I, vi–V.
  3. Retrogression moves backwards (V–IV, ii–vi, ♭VII–IV–I); a chord stream follows a stepwise bass.
  4. Embellishing chords are harmonized non-chord tones: passing, neighbor, arpeggiating, pedal (incl. the cadential ⁶₄).

Key ideas
  1. Major: W W H W W W H. Natural minor: W H W W H W W.
  2. Harmonic minor raises 7̂ (a leading tone); melodic minor raises 6̂ and 7̂ going up.
  3. Key signatures write a key's sharps or flats once, for every octave: sharps F C G D A E B, flats B E A D G C F.
  4. Relative keys share a key signature (C major, A minor); parallel keys share a tonic (C major, C minor).

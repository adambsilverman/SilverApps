Key ideas
  1. Move each voice to the nearest chord tone, favoring contrary motion.
  2. Double the root in root position; not the bass in first inversion; the bass in second inversion. Never double 7̂.
  3. Resolve 7̂ up to 8̂, 4̂ down to 3̂, and chordal 7ths down by step.
  4. Avoid parallel and consecutive P5s and P8s, direct 5ths/8ves in the outer voices, crossing, and gaps over an octave between upper voices.

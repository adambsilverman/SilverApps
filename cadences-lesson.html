Key ideas
  1. Authentic: V(⁷)–I. Perfect (PAC) = both root position, tonic in the soprano; otherwise imperfect (IAC).
  2. Half (HC): ends on V. Plagal half (PHC): ends on IV.
  3. Plagal (PC): IV–I. Deceptive (DC): V–vi (a tonic substitute).
  4. Closure: open = half cadences; weak = DC, IAC; closed = PAC, PC.
  5. Only at phrase endings: a V–I inside a phrase isn't a cadence. Besides V, an open cadence can end on IV, ♭VII, ♭VI, II, iv or Vsus4.

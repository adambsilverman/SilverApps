Drone.

Tune against a steady drone: one note, or a stack of notes. Compare equal temperament with just intonation (pure ratios from the harmonic series) and Pythagorean tuning (everything built from pure 3:2 fifths). Hear how just thirds are calm, Pythagorean thirds bright and wide, and how the same note changes when you tune from a different root.

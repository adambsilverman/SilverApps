Interactive Music Training

Multitrack Studio

Build a song in layers. Start with a backing track or a drum loop (send one from the Groove Maker or open an audio file), then add tracks: play a melody on a synth with the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard, or record yourself through the microphone. Mix the tracks and export the result. Everything stays on your device; export your song to keep it.

Editor

Mixer

Each channel's inserts run top to bottom before its fader. Select an insert to edit it; add effects with + FX. The Master channel's effects process the whole mix.

Effects

Mini synth

The synthesizer from Introduction to Pitch: Synthesizing pitch. Changes apply to every note on the track; play the keyboard below to hear them.

Vocal tuner

Groove Maker

Build a progression and groove, then press Add to the studio to drop it in as a backing track.

Drum machine

Click steps to build a loop; it repeats on the track from bar 1. Tap the pads (or press Q W E R / A S D F while a drum track is selected) to play live, and arm the track (●) and press Rec to record what you play.

Hold or record chords on a synth track and they play back as broken chords, in time with the tempo.

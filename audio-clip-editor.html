3. Export

WAV is uncompressed and lossless. MP3 is lossy but much smaller; MP3 files use 32, 44.1 or 48 kHz, so other rates are converted to 44.1 kHz first. MP3 encoding uses LAME (via lamejs, LGPL).

Learn more about sample rates, bit depth and formats in About Music Software.

Video Clip Editor

Open a video, trim it, crop it, split it into clips, and clean up its sound with normalization and simple mastering (presets, or set the compression, EQ and reverb yourself). Instagram It makes a vertical version from any part of the frame and any stretch of time. Your video never leaves your device: everything happens in this browser, and you save the results as files.

    3. Sound

    Output level

    Play the video to hear the settings. Normalizing measures the trimmed part's loudness and sets a gain so the result lands on the target; the limiter keeps peaks under the ceiling.

    4. Instagram It

    Choose the part of the frame and the stretch of time for a vertical (or square) Instagram version. Reels can run up to 3 minutes; most perform best between about 15 and 90 seconds.

    5. Export and save

    Video exports record in real time (a 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds), so keep this tab open and visible. Files save to your Downloads folder (on iPhone and iPad, to Files › Downloads). Instagram needs MP4: if your browser can only make WebM, use Safari or Chrome. MP3 encoding uses LAME (via lamejs, LGPL).

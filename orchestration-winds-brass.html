Key ideas
  1. Score order: flutes, oboes, clarinets, bassoons, then horns, trumpets, trombones, tuba. Auxiliaries sit next to their family by size.
  2. Valves: 1 lowers a M2, 2 a m2, 3 a m3; 1+3 a P4; 1+2+3 a tritone. The lips choose the partial.
  3. Mutes change color and volume; allow up to 10 seconds to put one in.

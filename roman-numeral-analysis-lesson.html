Quick reference
Key ideas
  1. Uppercase = major, lowercase = minor, ° diminished, + augmented.
  2. Figures: ⁶ and ⁶₄ for triad inversions; ⁷, ⁶₅, ⁴₃, ⁴₂ for seventh chords.
  3. ♭ / ♯ before the numeral: root lowered or raised from the major scale. / means 'of' (V/V).
  4. Transposing never changes the numerals.

Groove Maker

Build a progression from Roman numerals, drag the chords into order, then give it a groove: pick a style, a meter and separate instruments for the chords, bass and drums, and tap the boxes to change the beat. Choose the Beatbox kit (or the Beatbox style) to build the drum part from beatbox sounds, one sound per box. Change the key and every chord moves at once, because numerals describe chords by scale degree. Learn the labels in Introduction to Roman Numeral Analysis.

Key ideas
  1. Motive: a short, distinctive idea, labeled x, y, z.
  2. Transposition: real (exact intervals) or tonal (in the key). Inversion: upside down. Retrograde: backwards.
  3. Augmentation / diminution: longer / shorter values. Fragmentation: part of the motive, developed on its own.
  4. Sequence: a pattern repeated higher or lower; tonal, real, mixed or modified; melodic and/or harmonic.

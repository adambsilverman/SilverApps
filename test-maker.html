Test maker

Build a test from the Test Yourself question banks. Pick the units it covers and how many questions it has. Unlike Test Yourself, a test gives no hints and no links back to the lessons, and each question gets one answer: students can go back and change answers until they submit, and only then see their score.

Share the test as a link, or download it as a SCORM package for D2L Brightspace, Canvas, Blackboard or Moodle so scores go straight to your gradebook. Students outside an LMS can press Report for my professor when they finish; you check those reports on Check reports.

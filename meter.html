Key ideas
  1. Simple meter: the beat divides in two. Compound: the beat is dotted and divides in three (6/8 = 2 beats).
  2. Asymmetrical meters (5/8, 7/8) have unequal beats of 2 and 3. Mixed meter changes time signature.
  3. Beam by beat so the beat is always visible; older vocal music flags each syllable and beams only melismas.
  4. Pick-up: notes before the first downbeat. Syncopation: accents against the beat. Hemiola: three against two.

Ear Training Rhythm

Dictation: listen to a rhythm and write it down. Perform: read the rhythm, listen to the count-in, then perform it. Practice generated rhythms, rhythms from around the world, classical dance rhythms and drum rudiments, clap on cue while counting rests, or perform a phasing piece in the manner of Steve Reich's Clapping Music. Perform by tapping the pad or the space bar, or by clapping into the microphone. The app shows which notes were on time, early, late, missed or extra.

Your answer

Press Hear it (as often as you like), then build the rhythm with the buttons below. Only where each note starts is checked: with these short clicks, a longer note sounds the same as a shorter note followed by a rest.

Vocal Tuner

Pitch-correct a vocal the way Auto-Tune does, move it to another key, shift its formants, add harmonies, or play with voice effects. Your recording never leaves your device. Learn how pitch correction works in About Music Software: Editing and pitch correction.

1. A vocal clip

Drop a recording here or choose a file (WAV, MP3, M4A…). A dry solo voice works best: one singer, no instruments, little reverb. Up to 2 minutes.

Use headphones or a quiet room. Recording stops after 2 minutes.

2. Tune and transform

Transpose moves the pitch while keeping the voice's character (formants) in place. Formant shift changes the size of the "voice" without changing the pitch: up sounds smaller and brighter, down sounds bigger and darker. Chipmunk = pitch up + formants up.

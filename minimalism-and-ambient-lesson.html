Key ideas
  1. Phasing: identical patterns shift out of alignment and back.
  2. Additive: 1; 1 2; 1 2 3… Loops of lengths a and b realign after their least common multiple.
  3. Tintinnabuli: stepwise melody + a voice on one triad.

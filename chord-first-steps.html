Triads and Seventh Chords: First Steps

Identify a chord one small step at a time. Read each note from the bottom up, measure the distance between neighbors, find the root, check the 3rd, 5th (and 7th) above the root, then name the chord's quality and its position. When you're comfortable, try the Chord Figures and Symbols drills, or read the lesson.

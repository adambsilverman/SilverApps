Triads and Seventh Chords: First Steps

Two step-by-step activities: identify a chord you see, or spell a chord from its root, quality and position. To identify, you read each note from the bottom up, measure the distance between neighbors, find the root, check the 3rd, 5th (and 7th) above the root, then name the chord's quality and its position. To spell, you work from the root up: find each letter, count half steps, choose the accidental, then put the right note in the bass. When you're comfortable, try the Chord Figures and Symbols drills, or read the lesson.

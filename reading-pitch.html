Key ideas
  1. Treble lines E G B D F, spaces F A C E. Bass lines G B D F A, spaces A C E G.
  2. Alto lines F A C E G (middle C on the middle line). Tenor lines D F A C E (middle C on the 4th line).
  3. Landmarks: G4 (treble clef's curl), F3 (bass clef's dots), middle C (C clef's center).
  4. Alignment: simultaneous notes line up vertically; seconds put the higher note to the right; accidentals sit just before the notehead, highest closest to the chord.

Key ideas
  1. Spell each chord and compare the melody notes to it; notes outside the chord are non-chord tones (dissonances).
  2. Step–step: same direction = passing tone (PT); back again = neighbor tone (NT).
  3. Leap–step = appoggiatura (APP); step–leap = escape tone (ET).
  4. Held over then down a step = suspension (SUS); up a step = retardation (RET). Early = anticipation (ANT).

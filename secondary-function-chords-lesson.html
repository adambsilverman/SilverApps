Key ideas
  1. Name the chord (stack the notes in thirds; slash = bass note).
  2. Read its quality: major or 7 (dom7) → secondary dominant; °7 → secondary leading-tone; minor or ø7 → secondary pre-dominant.
  3. Find its target: dominant → a P4 above the root; leading-tone → a m2 above; pre-dominant → a M2 below.
  4. Add the figures for inversions: 6, 6/4 for triads; 7, 6/5, 4/3, 4/2 for seventh chords.

SilverApps for Music Training

Apps for review, practice and skill building in music theory, ear training and performance. Practice on your own with instant feedback, or run any trainer live with a whole class on student devices.

  • Modes Modes: The Simple TruthAn interactive lesson: the same notes from any degree of any major scale, parallel modes, major and minor families, PADMIL in any key, modal key signatures, naming modes by ear from scales and chord progressions, and modes beyond the diatonic.Interactive lessonOpen ▸
  • Secondary function Understanding Secondary Function ChordsAn interactive lesson: name the chord, read its quality, find its target and write the Roman numeral for secondary dominants, leading-tone chords and pre-dominants, with examples to play in any key.Interactive lessonOpen ▸

  • Voice leading & texture Understanding Voice Motion and PolyphonyAn interactive lesson: voice ranges, the four kinds of motion, forbidden parallels, spacing and crossing, and the textures of polyphony from drone and organum to canon and heterophony, with sound examples.Interactive lessonOpen ▸

Drills and Games

Every drill works three ways:

  • Practice soloOpen any app and start — it explains each answer and brings missed items back for review.
  • Host a class gameOn the projector, open an app and choose Host a class game. You pick each question; the class answers anonymously.
  • Join from any deviceOn a phone, tablet or laptop, open the same app, tap Join a game, and enter the room code (or scan the QR code).

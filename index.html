SilverApps Interactive Music Training

Apps for review, practice and skill building in music theory, ear training and performance. Each topic has an interactive lesson, drills with instant feedback, or both. Every drill can also run live as a class game on student devices.

For instructors: running a class game
  • Practice soloOpen any drill and start: it explains each answer and brings missed items back for review.
  • Host a class gameOn the projector, open a drill and choose Host a class game. You pick each question; the class answers anonymously.
  • Join from any deviceOn a phone, tablet or laptop, open the same drill, tap Join a game, and enter the room code (or scan the QR code).

Students can also make a completion report in any drill (“Your progress” panel). Check a student's report.

Skill Tests

Eight fundamentals to master. Each test is 20 fresh questions; 80% or better and the skill is mastered. Take a test as often as you like. Instructors: Gradebook Sync has a SCORM package for each, worth full credit once a student reaches mastery.

  • Skill Test 1

    Units of Rhythm

    How long each note and rest lasts, and which values add up to which.

    Lesson: Units of RhythmTest Yourself: Units of RhythmTake the test: Units of Rhythm
  • Skill Test 2

    Bar Completion

    Find the one note that completes a bar in simple meter (2/4, 3/4, 4/4) and in compound meter (6/8, 9/8, 12/8).

    Lesson: Bar CompletionTest Yourself: Bar CompletionTake the test: Bar Completion
  • Skill Test 3

    Notes on the Staff

    Name notes in treble and bass clef, on the staff and on ledger lines.

    Lesson: Notes on the StaffTest Yourself: Notes on the StaffTake the test: Notes on the Staff
  • Skill Test 4

    Intervals

    Name intervals on the staff (quality and number), and name the note a given interval above or below.

    Lesson: IntervalsTest Yourself: IntervalsTake the test: Intervals
  • Skill Test 5

    Major and Minor Scales

    Spell major scales and natural, harmonic and melodic minor scales, up to five sharps or flats.

    Lesson: Major and Minor ScalesTest Yourself: Major and Minor ScalesTake the test: Major and Minor Scales
  • Skill Test 6

    Key Signatures

    Name the major or minor key of a key signature, and the signature of a key, up to five sharps or flats.

    Lesson: Key SignaturesTest Yourself: Key SignaturesTake the test: Key Signatures
  • Skill Test 7

    Chord Spelling

    Spell triads (major, minor, augmented, diminished, sus4) and all eight kinds of seventh chord from chord symbols.

    Lesson: Chord SpellingTake the test: Chord Spelling
  • Skill Test 8

    Roman Numerals

    Give the diatonic Roman numeral for a chord symbol in a major or minor key.

    Lesson: Roman NumeralsTest Yourself: Roman NumeralsTake the test: Roman Numerals

Minimalist Process Lab

Write a short fragment, then let processes do the composing: canons, phasing, additive and subtractive growth, augmentation, retrograde, inversion, chord loops and drones, in up to six voices with different instruments. Learn the ideas in Understanding Minimalist and Ambient Music.

1. The fragment

Press a cell to add a note, drag to the right to hold it longer, press a note to remove it. Changing the key or scale moves the whole fragment.

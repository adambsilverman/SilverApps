Gradebook sync

Every lesson's Test Yourself quiz can send its score to your course gradebook. Download a SCORM package for a quiz here and add it to your course in D2L Brightspace, Canvas, Blackboard or Moodle. Students open the quiz from inside the course; when they finish, their first-try score (as a percent) goes into the gradebook. If they take it again, the gradebook keeps their best score.

The package is tiny: it holds no lesson content, just a page that shows the live quiz from this site and passes the score along. Updates to the lesson reach your students without a new upload. Step-by-step setup for each LMS is on About this site.

No LMS, or you'd rather not upload anything? Students can press Report for my professor at the end of any quiz and send you a report with a verification code. Paste a stack of them into Check reports to verify them and download a gradebook import file.

Download a SCORM package

The packages are SCORM 1.2, which every major LMS accepts. Upload each quiz's package separately; each one becomes its own gradebook item.
